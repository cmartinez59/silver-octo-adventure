# Travel Documents

Travel documents are necessary for a successful trip. Having the correct paperwork prepared ahead of time can help avoid delays and complications.

## Important Documents

### Identification

A government issued ID is necessary for many forms of travel and may be required when checking into hotels and boarding flights or renting a car.

### Passports

A passport is usually required for international travel. It is important to check expiration dates before making plans. 

### Staying Organized

I try to keep both digital and printed copies of important documents. This can be helpful if something becomes lost or damaged. 

### Documents I Keep

	- Passport
	- Drivers license
	- Flight confirmation
	- Hotel reservations
	- Emergency Contacts

### Preparing Before Departure

Checking document requirements ahead of time helps prevent last-minute surprises.

	> Being organized before a trip can make traveling easier and less stressful.
