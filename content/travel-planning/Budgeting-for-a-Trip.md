# Budgeting for a Trip

Creating a budget is one of the most important parts of planning a trip. A travel budget helps me understand how much I can spend and prevents unexpected financial stress while traveling.  Unless money is no object, a budget is necessary. 

## Travel Expenses

### Transportation

Transportation costs often include airfare, fuel, rental cars, parking, Uber/Lyft, and/or public transportation. These expenses can make up a larger portion of a travel budget. 

### Lodging

The costs of hotels, rentals, or resorts vary depending on location and travel season. Comparing prices can reduce expenses and being less particular where you lay your head at night can help lower costs. 

### Budgeting Tips

- Set a realistic spending limit.
-  Research prices ahead of time.
-  Save extra money for emergencies.
-  Compare transportation options.

### Tracking Spending

I like to keep track of expenses before and during a trip. This allows me to adjust my spending if needed. Typically, I try to set aside a certain amount I can spend per day. 

	> Sticking to a budget allows travelers to focus on enjoying their trip and less on worrying about money.
