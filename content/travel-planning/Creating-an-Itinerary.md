# Creating an Itinerary

An itinerary helps organize a trip by keeping important information in one place. I find that having a basic plan helps reduce stress and allows me to make better use of my time for traveling. Plus I can make sure I get to see all my favorite spots. 

## Benefits of an Itinerary

### Staying organized

An itinerary includes hotel information, transportation details, attraction tickets, and daily activities. Keeping these details together makes travel easier. 

### Saving Time

Planning activities ahead of time helps avoid wasting time deciding what to do each day.

### What I Include
	- Hotel information
	- Transportation Schedules
	- Activities and attractions
	- Emergency Contacts
	- Reservation confirmations

### Why I Use an Itinerary

I have found that using an itinerary helps me stay organized during a trip. Instead of trying to remember reservations and activities, I can keep everything in one place. It also helps me make better use of my time and avoid missing important attractions or events. 

### Flexibility is Important

Even with a plan, I leave room for changes. Weather conditions or unexpected opportunities may cause plans to change. 

	> A good itinerary provides structure while allowing flexibility.
