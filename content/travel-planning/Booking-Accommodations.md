# Booking Accommodations

Finding a comfortable and affordable place to stay is an important part of travel planning. Accommodations affect the overall travel experience and can have a major impact on a travel budget. 

## Types of Lodging

### Hotels

Hotels are popular because they provide convenience, privacy, and services such as housekeeping.

### Vacation Rentals

Vacation rentals can offer more space and may include kitchens, making them useful for longer trips.

### Hostels

Hostels are a popular option for travelers who want to save money and meet other travelers. 

### What I Look For

	- Location
	- Price
	- Cleanliness
	- Safety
	- Reviews
